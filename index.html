AV Decision Support Prototype

High-level driving recommendations from real NGSIM US-101 traffic, using Llama 3.1 8B with a rule-based safety filter.

1. Choose or describe a situation

Picking a scenario fills in the form. You can then change any value to try your own version.

Llama runs on this laptop's CPU, so an answer takes about 30 seconds.

2. Recommendation

Choose a scenario or fill in the form, then press Get recommendation.
Research prototype: the recommendation is high-level decision support, not control of a vehicle. Scenarios come from NGSIM US-101 (US Department of Transportation). The safety filter, the labels and the automatic check are rules written for this project.
